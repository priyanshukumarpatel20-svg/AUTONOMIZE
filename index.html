            How this dashboard works

            Scores are computed locally by your backend at https://demo.invalid. The dashboard polls every 30 seconds and keeps showing the last known data if the connection drops.

            Writing, assessment and AI-assistant categories are each tracked separately, so a graded assessment is never scored against the same baseline as ordinary drafting.

            Site totals count tracked time only. Time spent in a tab that is open but idle is excluded from every figure on this page.

            Only counts and timings leave your device. Raw keystrokes and document text are never transmitted, and your profile photo stays in this browser.

            Autonomize · Independence measured against your own baseline, never a class average.
